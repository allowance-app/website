About allowance

Allowance is a financial wellness app that helps you make better decisions by providing more useful data about the future. It eliminates the cognitive load required by traditional banking and budgeting apps when trying to decide if (or when) you can afford something. It provides a better "back of the napkin", allowing users to create a plan for anything, with an interface that's just as fast.

Every day, we make major and minor decisions that affect our financial wellness with very limited information. We check our balance, try to remember where that money is already going, and make a best guess.

Allowance fills this gap by performing these calculations for you, telling you what you can safely spend and protecting your money from yourself. It provides an "Actually Available Balance", that accounts for future income and expenses, setting funds aside so you don’t even have to think about them.

Allowance is a day planner for money: Gain financial awareness using a calendar with recurring or one time expenses. Get an ongoing projected balance to help you plan for future projects.

The app intelligently prioritizes information, alerting you to potential shortfalls or telling you exactly how long your current funds must last.

Use allowance for anything: a personal budget, a household, a business, property management, a house, a vacation, plan for college, a startup investment, or just trying to see if you can get the fancy coffee.
